How does an Arlington Heights home change the plan?

The village has a wide mix of housing: one-story ranches, split-levels and raised ranches, two-story family homes, and condominiums and townhomes. Each layout raises its own questions.

Ranches

Bedrooms and bathroom on one floor is the easiest setup for aging in place. A walk-in tub in the hall bath is often a direct swap for a 60-inch tub. We check the hallway turn into the bathroom, since a boxed tub needs a clear path.

Split-levels and raised ranches

The full bath is often up a half-flight or a full flight from the entry. That matters twice: for carrying in the tub, and for whether the bather can keep using those stairs. If stairs are already hard, the safest fixture may belong on the main level.

Two-story homes

Primary bathrooms upstairs are common. Many families put the walk-in tub or roll-in shower in the upstairs bath now and plan a first-floor option for later. Measure the stair width and the landing turn before choosing a model.

Condos and townhomes

Associations usually set rules for contractor hours, elevator use, parking and insurance certificates. Units with shared plumbing stacks may need board approval before a plumber opens a wall. Ask management what they need before scheduling.

Do I need a permit in Arlington Heights?

Arlington Heights runs permits through its Building & Life Safety Department at Village Hall, 33 S. Arlington Heights Road. The village says all initial building permit submittals must now be made electronically through its Civic Access Portal. The department's phone is 847-368-5560.

Whether a straight tub swap needs a plumbing permit, and what the inspection involves, is the village's call, not ours or a website's. Third-party "permit guides" for Arlington Heights often copy Chicago rules by mistake. Ask the department directly, or let your installer handle it and show you the permit.

Whoever does the work, Illinois licenses plumbers, so ask for the license number of the person doing the plumbing. A permit on file also helps if you later sell the home.

Permit checklist

  • Ask who files the permit and who meets the inspector
  • Get the plumber's Illinois license number
  • Confirm electrical work for heaters or jets
  • Condo? Get association approval in writing

Arlington Heights Senior Center

The village owns and runs the building, and partner agencies deliver services there, including Catholic Charities senior services, volunteer transportation through Connections to Care, meals through Meals on Wheels Northern Illinois, health programs, the library and the park district. It serves people 55 and over with no membership or residency requirement. See the Senior Center page and its senior resources list.

Wheeling Township

Wheeling Township runs from Lake Cook Road on the north to Central Road on the south, between the Des Plaines River and Wilke Road, so many Arlington Heights homes north of Central Road are in it. Homes south of Central Road or west of Wilke Road are in other townships, so check your tax bill. The township office is on North Arlington Heights Road; see wheelingtownship.com.

North West Housing Partnership

NWHP lists Arlington Heights among the towns served by CAPABLE, its aging-in-place program for people 60 and older or adults with disabilities, and the village's senior resources page lists its senior handyman program. NWHP is also a Cook County grantee for the state's HRAP accessibility grants. Start at nwhp.net.

Cook County property taxes

The Assessor's Home Improvement Exemption keeps up to $75,000 of added value off your taxes for up to four years, applied automatically. Owners 65 and older may also qualify for the Senior Exemption. See the Assessor's exemptions page.

Is there help paying for a walk-in tub in Arlington Heights?

Most walk-in tubs are paid for privately. Original Medicare generally does not cover them, and the public programs we checked tend to fund roll-in showers, grab bars and repairs rather than tubs. That said, a few routes are worth a call before you decide:

  • HRAP: up to $50,000 for accessibility and safety repairs for single-family owners at or below 80 percent of area median income, through NWHP. Waitlists are common.
  • VA HISA: lifetime limit of $6,800 or $2,000 for medically necessary changes, with a VA prescription and approval before work. See the HISA guide.
  • Medical expense deduction: the IRS can treat medically necessary home changes as a deduction above 7.5 percent of income, minus any increase in home value. Ask a tax preparer.

Arlington Heights questions

Do you install walk-in tubs in Arlington Heights?

Yes. We serve Arlington Heights and the surrounding northwest suburbs. Many straight swaps of a 60-inch tub finish in a day once the model, drain side and delivery path are confirmed.

Do I need a village permit for a walk-in tub in Arlington Heights?

Ask the Building & Life Safety Department at 847-368-5560, or have your installer handle it. The village says initial building permit submittals are made electronically through its Civic Access Portal.

Where can Arlington Heights seniors borrow a shower chair?

The Arlington Heights Nurses' Club runs a Lending Closet at the Senior Center, 1801 W. Central Road. Call 847-797-5315.

Who helps Arlington Heights seniors with grab bars and small repairs?

North West Housing Partnership lists Arlington Heights in its CAPABLE aging-in-place program for people 60 and older or adults with disabilities, and the village lists its senior handyman program. Call 847-969-0561.

Is there a grant for bathroom accessibility in Arlington Heights?

The state's HRAP program offers up to $50,000 for accessibility and safety repairs to single-family owners at or below 80 percent of area median income, through grantees such as North West Housing Partnership. Waitlists are common.

Is a walk-in tub or a roll-in shower better for a split-level home?

It depends on who bathes and whether stairs are still manageable. If the bathroom is up a flight and stairs are getting hard, a main-level shower may be safer long term. If stairs are fine and the person loves to soak, a walk-in tub upstairs works well.
