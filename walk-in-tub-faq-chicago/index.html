Everyday use

How long does a bath take in a walk-in tub?

Plan for a few extra minutes compared with a standard tub, because you sit inside while it fills and stay seated while it drains. Fast-fill faucets and fast-drain systems shorten both. Many people find the soak itself is the part they look forward to.

Can I shower in a walk-in tub instead of taking a bath?

Absolutely. A handheld sprayer comes with most models, which means a seated rinse is possible any day you do not feel like waiting for a fill. Some models also offer an overhead shower option.

How do I clean it?

Rinse and wipe after each use with a soft cloth and a non-abrasive cleaner suitable for the tub's surface. Give the rubber door gasket a gentle wipe so grime does not keep it from closing tightly. Jetted models have their own cleaning routines, so follow the manufacturer's instructions.

What should I do if water appears near the door?

First make sure the door was fully latched and the seal is clean. If water still appears, stop using the tub and call your installer. Small seal or leveling issues are easiest to fix early.

Is it okay to stand on the seat or rim?

No. Do not stand on the seat or the rim. Keep toiletries within reach in a caddy or niche so nobody climbs or stretches.

How often should the door seal be replaced?

It depends on the manufacturer and how the tub is used and cleaned. Keep it clean, inspect it now and then and follow the warranty terms. Call your installer if you see water near the door.

Can a walk-in tub be moved if I sell my home?

Technically it can be removed, but it is installed as a permanent fixture and the space would then need a new tub or shower. Most people leave it in place.

Do walk-in tubs need special cleaners?

Use non-abrasive cleaners suitable for the tub's surface, usually acrylic or fiberglass, and follow the manufacturer's guidance for jet systems. Avoid scouring pads that scratch.

Will a walk-in tub help with arthritis?

Many people with arthritis find warm water soothing, and a low step and seated bathing make entry easier on hips and knees. Ask your doctor what water temperature and soak time are right for you.

Can a walk-in tub be installed where a shower stall is now?

Sometimes, if the space is long enough. Many walk-in tubs need an alcove close to a standard tub size. Shorter spaces may suit a compact model or an accessible shower conversion instead.

Chicago buildings

I live upstairs in a two-flat. What about the neighbors below?

A properly installed walk-in tub with a good door seal should not leak. Because water damage in a two-flat affects the unit below, it pays to choose an experienced installer, keep the seal clean and fix any small drip right away. Let your downstairs neighbor know when the work is scheduled.

Are walk-in tubs allowed in Chicago condos and co-ops?

Usually, with approval. Most associations want a scope of work, the contractor's license and insurance certificate, and elevator and work-hour arrangements before plumbing work starts. Start that paperwork early.

I rent my apartment. Can I get a walk-in tub?

You would need the owner's signed consent, because the tub becomes part of the building. Under the federal Fair Housing Act, landlords must generally allow reasonable modifications for tenants with disabilities, usually paid for by the tenant. Get any agreement on paper.

Is a filled walk-in tub too heavy for an old wood floor?

Walk-in tubs hold more water than standard tubs, so a filled tub with a bather is heavy. Most residential floors handle it, but in older homes we check floor framing under the bathroom, especially if the tile shows cracks or the floor feels soft.

Family, guests and resale

Does a walk-in tub change resale value?

Buyers differ. An accessible bath appeals to some house hunters, while others would rather see a conventional tub. Where a home has two bathrooms, leaving one conventional covers both audiences.

Can grandchildren use it?

Only with an adult right there. The basin is taller and the seat raised, and any tub holding water calls for constant supervision of little ones.

Can two people bathe at once?

Two-seat models exist, but they need a bigger room and a bigger water heater. The typical Chicago bathroom is a better match for a single-seat tub.

Power, storms and safety

What happens if the power goes out?

Anything mechanical keeps working: the latch, the seat and a drain that relies on gravity. Anything electric does not, including jets and heaters, and an electric water heater will not recover. Skip long soaks until ComEd restores power, and keep a flashlight handy.

Is it safe to bathe during a thunderstorm?

No. Weather Service guidance is to stay off plumbing while lightning is around, since current can travel through pipes. Bathe after the storm moves through.

Can I open the door if I feel unwell?

The door is designed to stay sealed while there is water in the tub. Open the drain and wait for it to empty. That is one reason fast drains matter, and why anyone with heart or blood pressure concerns should ask their doctor about soak time and temperature.

Buying with confidence

A salesperson wants me to sign today for a discount. Should I?

Slow down. Any reputable installer will let you sleep on it, involve your family and gather other bids. Urgency manufactured by a salesperson is a warning sign.

What if I regret signing during an in-home sales visit?

Federal rules help here. For many in-home sales, the Federal Trade Commission allows a three-business-day window to back out, though some transactions are excluded. Check the FTC page linked below and the cancellation terms in your paperwork.

Do you only sell one brand?

No. We work with several manufacturers, including Ella's Bubbles, and help you compare models across brands based on fit, features and budget.

Can I buy just the tub?

Yes. DIY drop-ship delivery is available nationwide, so your own licensed contractor can install on your schedule.
